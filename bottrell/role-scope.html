Role scope suggestion

Download this page as PDF

Proposed working title: Business Systems & Growth Lead. The remit should be agreed before the title. This is a proposal for discussion, rather than an agreed position.

I want responsibility for improving how the business works as a whole. My strongest interest is reviewing, then building and improving systems, and using evidence to check whether they work. Marketing, IT, coding and development, finance administration and building operations can all contribute to that remit.

I am willing to build towards a senior, challenging role, earning trust through results and taking on greater authority over time. A 90-day review would assess results and agree how responsibilities and decision-making authority should develop.

Areas I could own

Area Proposed responsibility
Systems and workflows Map work from enquiry to delivery, identify repeated tasks and unclear ownership, and implement agreed improvements.
Automation and development Select useful tasks, deliver and test small pilots, document their use, then extend to larger workflows as my knowledge of the systems develops.
Client experience Improve enquiry routing, handovers, complaint handling and follow-up; establish clear remedies and escalation limits.
Growth and marketing Keep business information consistent, improve routes to the right service and test activities against suitable enquiries and delivery capacity.
Staff onboarding Maintain the shared induction, role-specific pathways, readiness checklists and improvement reporting; coordinate guidance on representing the business consistently.
Finance administration and building operations Coordinate agreed processes, records, service issues and handovers where they affect the wider operation.
Owner reporting Make priorities, progress, results and decisions requiring owner input easy to review.

The role would cover the businesses and services Rebecca confirms are currently active. Each legal provider and its professional responsibilities would remain clear.

Decision boundaries

  • Routine decisions: progressively delegate defined workflow changes, client remedies and operational decisions within agreed limits.
  • Owner or designated approval: larger spending, major system changes, commitments outside the remit and exceptions to agreed limits.
  • Qualified service leads: professional advice, technical standards, technical teaching and approval of work requiring their expertise.
  • Designated trainers and supervisors: day-to-day technical training and supervision, supported by the onboarding system I coordinate.

One clear reporting line, early feedback and protected improvement time would let me work independently and reduce routine management and checking. I can help train people when needed; continual basic staff supervision should remain a limited part of the role.

First 90 days

Period Proposed outputs
Days 1–30 Confirm the active business scope, priorities and decision limits. Review the current systems, client journey and onboarding process. Establish a baseline and complete approved quick wins.
Days 31–60 Improve one priority workflow, evaluate an automation pilot, clarify business-information ownership and report progress. Address agreed gaps in onboarding materials or readiness checks.
Days 61–90 Review the evidence, recommend what to expand or change, and run one approved marketing experiment where appropriate. Hold the 90-day review of results, responsibilities and authority; agree the next quarter.

Targets should follow the baseline. Useful measures include response times, completed handovers and follow-ups, rework, demonstrated time saved and staff readiness. Growth measures also need actual enquiries, engagements and delivery capacity.
